The plant

A 22 kW IGBT inverter of our own design driving a 15 kW submersible set — a Chetak 6C-20A motor on an eight-stage Marshal Gold SP 60 — 70 m down a well, supplied directly from a 21.96 kWp LONGi Hi-MO 7 array with no battery.

Checking the numbers

The shot tallies in the paper can be recounted from the relay box's own record. Counting the COMBINED marks in logs/2026091[014]/marks.log gives 14 shots with the droop enabled and 11 without, which is the 0/14 against 10/11 of Section X. Binaries carry their CRC-32 in the filename and can be verified against it.
